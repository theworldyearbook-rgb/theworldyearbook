🌍 Our Global Mission

The first real yearbook
for the entire planet.

The World Yearbook is a simple idea: what if every person on Earth had a spot? Not a social media profile. Not a follower count. Just a tile. Your face. Your name. Your moment in 2026 — documented forever alongside millions of others.

We’re building something that has never existed. Started by two people with an idea and a domain name. The goal is one million humans, one yearbook.

📌 Remember your tile number

Every submission gets a permanent tile number. The lower the number, the earlier you were here. Founding members (tiles #1–5,000) will always be marked — even after the free period closes. Your number is your place in history.
